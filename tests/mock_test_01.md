### Question 5 [Topic: Profit, Loss & Discount]
A dishonest trader marks up the price of his goods by $40\%$ above the cost price and offers a discount of $25\%$ on the marked price. Furthermore, he cheats both the wholesaler and his customers: while purchasing, he takes $1100\text{ g}$ instead of $1000\text{ g}$ from the wholesaler, and while selling, he gives only $800\text{ g}$ instead of $1000\text{ g}$ to the customer. What is his overall profit percentage?

- (A) $40.5\%$
- (B) $42.25\%$
- (C) $44.375\%$
- (D) $48.125\%$

### Question 6 [Topic: Simple & Compound Interest]
A sum of money was invested at $r\%$ per annum simple interest for 3 years. Had it been invested at $(r + 4)\%$ per annum simple interest, it would have fetched ₹1,800 more. If the same sum is invested at $r\%$ per annum compound interest, compounded annually for 2 years, it amounts to ₹18,150. What would be the compound interest on the same sum for 2 years at $(r + 5)\%$ per annum, compounded annually?

- (A) ₹4,650.00
- (B) ₹4,837.50
- (C) ₹5,125.00
- (D) ₹5,250.50

### Question 7 [Topic: Time, Speed & Distance]

- (A) 360 m
- (B) 380 m
- (C) 400 m
- (D) 420 m

### Question 8 [Topic: Time & Work]

- (A) 36 days
- (B) 40 days
- (C) 45 days
- (D) 60 days

### Question 9 [Topic: Mixture & Alligation]

Then, $x$ litres of the resulting mixture is again withdrawn and replaced with water. If the ratio of milk to water in the container is now $27 : 37$, what is the value of $x$?

- (A) 15 litres
- (B) 18 litres
- (C) 20 litres
- (D) 24 litres
