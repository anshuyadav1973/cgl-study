# 60-Day Master Study Plan: SSC CGL General Awareness (GA)

## 1. Executive Summary & Strategy
General Awareness in SSC CGL is notoriously unpredictable, but the **TCS examination pattern** follows a distinct Pareto Principle ($80/20$ rule):
- **80% of questions come from ~20% of repeating themes**: Static GK, Indian Polity, Modern History, Biology, Indian Rivers/Physiography, Five-Year Plans, and Recent Current Affairs (Awards, Sports, Appointments, Schemes).
- **Target Score**: 35+ in Tier-1 (out of 50) and 45+ in Tier-2 (out of 75).
- **Daily Time Commitment**: 2.5 to 3 hours per day.

## 4. Month 1: Building High-ROI Foundations (Days 1–30)

### Week 1: Indian Polity + Foundation Static GK
- **Day 1**: Historical Background & Making of Constitution, Sources borrowed from other countries, Preamble. *(Static: Classical & Folk Dances of India - State-wise Part 1)*
- **Day 2**: Parts & Schedules, Union & Its Territory, Citizenship. *(Static: Folk Dances Part 2 + State Festivals Part 1)*
- **Day 3**: **Fundamental Rights (Articles 12–35)** — *Highest Yield!* Exceptions, Writs under Article 32 & 226. *(Static: State Festivals Part 2)*
- **Day 4**: **DPSP (Articles 36–51)** and **Fundamental Duties (51A)**. *(Static: Musical Instruments & Renowned Maestros/Gharanas)*
- **Day 5**: The President, Vice-President, Governor — Powers, Pardoning powers, Ordinances. *(Static: Census 2011 Key Data - Sex ratio, Literacy, Density)*
- **Day 6**: Parliament (Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha), Money Bill vs Financial Bill, Parliamentary Sessions & Terms. *(Static: National Parks & Wildlife Sanctuaries)*
- **Day 7**: Supreme Court, High Courts, Emergency Provisions, Key Amendments (42nd, 44th, 61st, 73rd, 74th, 86th, 101st-106th). **Weekly Revision + 100 Polity PYQs**.

### Week 2: Modern Indian History + Static GK
- **Day 8**: Advent of Europeans, Carnatic & Anglo-Mysore / Anglo-Maratha Wars summary. *(Static: UNESCO World Heritage Sites in India)*
- **Day 9**: **Revolt of 1857** (Centers, Leaders, British Suppressors, Causes, Outcomes). *(Static: Ramsar Wetland Sites & Biosphere Reserves)*
- **Day 10**: Governor-Generals & Viceroys and landmark policies (Subsidiary Alliance, Doctrine of Lapse, Vernacular Press Act). *(Static: First in India - Male & Female)*
- **Day 11**: Socio-Religious Reform Movements (Brahmo Samaj, Arya Samaj, Satya Shodhak Samaj, Ramakrishna Mission, founders, publications). *(Static: Important Temples & Architecture)*
- **Day 12**: **Indian National Congress (INC) Sessions** (1885, 1906, 1907, 1916, 1920, 1924, 1925, 1929, 1931, 1938, 1946). *(Static: Famous Stadiums, Trophies & Sports Terms)*
- **Day 13**: **Gandhian Phase (1915–1947)**: Champaran, Kheda, Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience, Quit India, Round Table Conferences, Cabinet Mission. *(Static: Famous Autobiographies & Books of Sports/Historical Personalities)*
- **Day 14**: Revolutionary Movements (HSRA, INA, Bhagat Singh, Subhash Chandra Bose) + Important British Acts (1909, 1919, 1935). **Weekly Revision + 100 History PYQs**.

### Week 3: Biology + Static GK
- **Day 15**: Cell Structure, Plant Cell vs Animal Cell, Organelles (Mitochondria, Ribosome, Lysosome, Nucleus). *(Static: Major River Valley Projects & Dams)*
- **Day 16**: **Vitamins & Deficiency Diseases** (Chemical names of Vit A, B-complex, C, D, E, K). *(Static: Thermal, Nuclear & Solar Power Plants in India)*
- **Day 17**: Human Digestive System, Enzymes, Nutrition, Respiratory System. *(Static: Major Mountain Passes & Peaks of India)*
- **Day 18**: Circulatory System (Blood groups, Rh factor, Heart anatomy, RBC/WBC/Platelets). *(Static: Boundary lines, International Borders & Neighboring Countries)*
- **Day 19**: Endocrine System (Major Glands, Hormones like Insulin, Thyroxine, Adrenaline, Pituitary hormones). *(Static: Important Days & Themes of the Year)*
- **Day 20**: Human Pathogenic Diseases (Bacterial, Viral, Fungal, Protozoan vectors) + Plant Kingdom basics (Bryophyta, Thallophyta, Pteridophyta, Angiosperms). *(Static: Major Ports & Airports)*
- **Day 21**: Ecology, Food Chain, Trophic levels, Air & Water pollution. **Weekly Revision + 100 Biology PYQs**.

### Week 4: Indian Geography + Static GK
- **Day 22**: Physiography of India (Himalayan Ranges, Trans-Himalayas, Eastern & Western Ghats, Coastal Plains, Islands). *(Static: Highest, Longest, Largest Superlatives in India & World)*
- **Day 23**: **Drainage System I**: Himalayan Rivers (Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra and their complete left/right bank tributaries). *(Static: Major Research Institutes in India)*
- **Day 24**: **Drainage System II**: Peninsular Rivers (Godavari, Krishna, Cauvery, Narmada, Tapi, Mahanadi) + Waterfalls & Lakes. *(Static: Famous Tribes of India state-wise)*
- **Day 25**: Indian Monsoon system, Western Disturbances, Climate classifications. *(Static: Classical Languages of India & Official Language provisions)*
- **Day 26**: Soils of India (Alluvial, Black/Regur, Red, Laterite characteristics) & Natural Vegetation. *(Static: Nicknames of Indian Cities)*
- **Day 27**: Agriculture (Kharif, Rabi, Zaid crops, Major producing states) & Minerals distribution (Iron ore, Bauxite, Coal, Mica belts). *(Static: Central Government Flagship Schemes - Part 1)*
- **Day 28**: Earth’s Interior, Volcanoes, Earthquakes, Atmosphere layers (Troposphere to Exosphere). **Weekly Revision + 100 Geography PYQs**.

## 5. Month 2: Completing Core, Current Affairs & Intensive PYQ Drills (Days 31–60)

### Week 5: Ancient & Medieval History + Chemistry
- **Day 29**: Indus Valley Civilization (Major sites, discoveries) & Vedic Age (Rigveda to Atharvaveda, Vedic terms). *(Static: Central Govt Schemes - Part 2)*
- **Day 30**: Buddhism & Jainism (Councils, Tirthankaras, Doctrines) + Mahajanapadas & Mauryan Empire (Ashoka’s Inscriptions).
- **Day 31**: Gupta Empire ("Golden Age", Navratnas, literature) + Post-Gupta (Harshavardhana) & Sangam Dynasty basics.
- **Day 32**: Delhi Sultanate (Slave, Khilji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi - Administration, architecture, market reforms of Alauddin).
- **Day 33**: Mughal Empire (Babur to Aurangzeb, land revenue systems, monuments) + Vijayanagara Empire & Marathas.
- **Day 34**: Chemistry: Modern Periodic Table (Trends in Atomic Radius, Electronegativity, Ionization energy), Acids, Bases & Salts (pH scale).
- **Day 35**: Common Chemical Names & Formulas (Baking soda, Washing soda, Bleaching powder, Plaster of Paris, Epsom salt, Quicklime), Metals & Alloys. **Weekly Revision + PYQ Practice**.

### Week 6: Physics + Economics
- **Day 36**: Physics: SI Units, Dimensions, Newton’s Laws, Motion, Work, Energy & Power formulas.
- **Day 37**: Gravitation (Variation of 'g'), Pressure, Surface Tension, Viscosity, Heat & Thermodynamics basics.
- **Day 38**: Optics (Convex/Concave mirrors and lenses, Total Internal Reflection, Scattering of light, Human Eye defects).
- **Day 39**: Electricity (Ohm's Law, Series/Parallel resistance), Magnetism, Sound & Waves (Doppler effect, Ultrasonic waves).
- **Day 40**: Economics: **Five-Year Plans** (1st to 12th: Models, targets, achievements — *High SSC Weightage!*).
- **Day 41**: National Income aggregates (GDP, GNP, NNP, Real vs Nominal GDP) + Inflation (CPI, WPI, Demand-pull, Cost-push).
- **Day 42**: Banking System & RBI Monetary Policy tools (Repo, Reverse Repo, CRR, SLR) + Budget & Taxes (GST, Direct vs Indirect, Deficits). **Weekly Revision + Economics PYQs**.

### Week 7: Current Affairs Marathon (Last 8–10 Months)
- **Day 43**: Sports Current Affairs: Olympics/Paralympics, Asian Games, Grand Slams, Cricket World Cup, National Games.
- **Day 44**: Major National & International Awards: Bharat Ratna, Padma Awards, Khel Ratna, Sahitya Akademi, Nobel Prizes, Oscars, National Film Awards.
- **Day 45**: Important Appointments: CJI, CEC, CAG, Attorney General, Chiefs of Defence, Heads of ISRO/DRDO/SEBI/RBI, International heads (UN, World Bank, IMF).
- **Day 46**: Military Exercises (Bilateral & Multilateral) + International Summits (G20, BRICS, SCO, ASEAN, COP).
- **Day 47**: Science & Tech Current Affairs: ISRO missions (Chandrayaan, Aditya-L1, Gaganyaan), Defense missile tests, Supercomputers.
- **Day 48**: Major Central & State Government Welfare Schemes, Portals, and Mobile Apps launched in the past year.
- **Day 49**: Indexes & Rankings (India’s rank in Global Innovation, Press Freedom, Hunger, Human Development, Cleanliness). **Weekly Full-length Sectional Mock**.

### Week 8: Mock Testing, Revision & High-Yield Consolidation
- **Day 50**: Comprehensive Revision: Articles 1–51A, Key Amendments & Writs.
- **Day 51**: Comprehensive Revision: Classical Dances, Folk Dances & Gharanas/Instruments.
